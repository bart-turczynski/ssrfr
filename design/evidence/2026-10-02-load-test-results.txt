## Conclusion

!57's fix holds. Under the bounded protocol the start-timeout class fails
with SSRFR_TEST_START_TIMEOUT=3000 (5/10 runs at 16x) and does not fail with
the default (0/10 at 16x, 0/20 at 1x and 2x). 1x and 2x of the cap are too
light to reproduce it: a proof of a start-timeout change needs about 16x.
The raw-server class never appeared outside the probe: 0 readiness timeouts
in 2,223 raw-server starts across the cells above, slowest 4.40 s (16x)
against the 20 s wait (SSRF-ptkasofy).
